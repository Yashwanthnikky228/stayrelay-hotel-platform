# StayRelay canonical planning sources

**Planning baseline:** 2026-10-09 audit set  
**Roadmap:** v3, 180 tasks; dependency graph reported verified by the roadmap workbook  
**Repository implementation truth:** this Git repository and its reviewed commits. Planning documents describe intent; they do not prove legal approval, provider acceptance, hotel relationships, live inventory, or pilot outcomes.

## Precedence

Use sources in this order when making implementation decisions:

1. The audited **Master Index and Current Plan**, **Final Pending Task List & Project Roadmap v3**, and **Complete Business Platform Master Blueprint** establish current direction, sequencing, and cross-domain coverage.
2. The **Master Technical and Interactive Design Specification**, current **UI/UX Design Blueprint** and **2026-10-09 UI/UX Audit**, and audited **Volumes 01–10** control the relevant product and domain details for a task.
3. The **Master Control Index** and **Platform Control Matrix** provide navigation, source precedence, workflow, role, notification, exception, integration, and data-domain controls.
4. **Volume 11 — Pre-Mortem & AI Execution Strategy** is the mandatory execution-safety protocol. **Volume 12 — Complete Business Platform Master Blueprint & Comprehensive Audit** is the cross-domain integration layer and does not replace domain-owning volumes.
5. Raw research, original dossier copies, duplicate pasted research, and the interactive prototype are historical or design-reference material. They do not override audited sources unless an audited decision explicitly promotes them.

When a conflict is found, record it with the source paths and defer a decision to the accountable owner. Do not silently resolve conflicts or infer business, legal, financial, risk, state-machine, or provider semantics. External claims require current evidence from the responsible authoritative source.
